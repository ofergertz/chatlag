Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

We collect nothing and we track nothing. ChatLag has no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no advertising SDK. Messages, names and game moves exist only in your phone's memory while the app is open, travel only phone to phone over Bluetooth to the passengers you are chatting or playing with, and are never uploaded, stored, or shared with us or anyone else.

Children and teens

Kids, teens and adults are kept in separate cabins. Kids' and teens' chats are screened on the phone for the patterns of grooming, in seven languages; nothing is sent anywhere to be checked. Children can be reached only by verified family and people a parent approves, and a teenager's private chat opens only with someone they have met in person. Every chat has Block and Report. No filter catches everything, so it is one guard among several. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because we collect none.

Permissions

Bluetooth: to find and talk to nearby passengers. Location (older Android only): Android requires it for Bluetooth scanning; ChatLag never reads or sends your location. Local network / Internet: only to reach the airline's server on the aircraft Wi-Fi. No camera, microphone or contacts.

Deleting your data

There is nothing to delete on our side. Close the app and everything you wrote is gone. To remove the parent PIN, unlock Kid Mode or uninstall the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: see Support.
