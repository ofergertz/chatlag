Child safety standards

ChatLag has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). It is forbidden to use ChatLag to:

  • contact, groom or sexually exploit a minor;
  • ask a minor for photos, personal details, a meeting, or a move to another app;
  • share or request child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form.

Anyone who does this will be blocked, and we will cooperate fully with the authorities.

How ChatLag is built to protect minors

  • ChatLag sends text and game moves only. It does not send photos or videos.
  • Adults and teenagers are kept in separate rooms. Children up to 12 can only be reached by verified family and by people a parent approves with their PIN.
  • A teenager's private chat opens only with someone they met in person.
  • Children's and teens' chats are screened on the phone for the patterns of grooming. No filter catches everything, so it is not the only guard.
  • Every chat has Block and Report. Reporting blocks the other person at once.

How to report

  • In the app: open the chat, tap the Safety menu, then Report. The person is blocked immediately. Because ChatLag works offline and keeps no server, the report stays on your phone and is not sent to us.
  • To us: email ofer1g38@gmail.com with "Child safety" in the subject. We answer within 48 hours.
  • To the authorities: if a child is in danger, contact the police or the flight crew right away. In Israel, call 105 (the National Child Online Protection Unit). In the US, report to NCMEC at report.cybertip.org. Elsewhere, contact your local police or hotline.

What we do with a report

ChatLag stores no messages, names or accounts on any server, so we cannot see or retrieve conversations. When we receive a report or any notice of CSAE, we:

  • review it and act on what is in our control, including updating the app's safety rules;
  • report confirmed CSAM to NCMEC or the relevant national authority, as the law requires;
  • cooperate with law enforcement requests.
